Simulate this project on https://wokwi.com

This lab has no published Wokwi project link yet. To recreate it in the
browser: open a new ESP32 (Arduino) project, paste sketch/sketch.ino into the
code tab and diagram.json into the diagram tab, set SIM_WOKWI to 1 in the
sketch, then press Start.

The Wokwi part library has no L293D or DC motor, and no DHT11. See the
"Simulating in Wokwi" section of README.md for the substitutions the diagram
makes and what they do and do not demonstrate.
